FAQ

Will these fit my BMW?+

If your door has the factory 2-pin puddle light connector, it plugs straight in. Check the full table on our Fitment page, or type your chassis code there for an instant answer.

Do I need to cut any wires?+

No. The unit plugs into the same OEM 2-pin connector your factory puddle light already uses. No cutting, no splicing, no soldering.

What does shipping cost?+

Free to Canada and the US. US orders arrive with no duties or customs fees to pay at delivery. We don't ship to other countries yet.

What if a unit arrives faulty?+

Every kit carries a 12-month warranty. Email info@mdivisionlighting.com with your order number and we'll sort out a replacement.

How long does install take?+

About 5 minutes for a 2-piece kit, 10 for a 4-piece, with a basic trim tool. See the full install guide.

Can I pick up locally instead of shipping?+

Yes — choose local pickup at checkout. We'll email you to arrange a time and public meeting spot in Vancouver, BC.

How does the free install offer work?+

The first 10 customers who choose local pickup and opt in get a free install from us, in exchange for a short clip of the install for our page. Spots are confirmed after checkout, first come first served.
