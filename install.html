Install guide

Three moves. Nothing to cut.

No wiring diagrams, no splicing kits, no trip to a shop. About 5 minutes for a 2-piece kit, 10 for a 4-piece, with basic hand tools.

5:00
Minutes left to finish
01

Unclip the factory light.

A plastic trim tool under the door edge pops the stock puddle light straight out of its housing. No screws, no adhesive to fight.

02

Plug into the same 2-pin connector.

The factory harness clicks straight into our unit — the same OEM 2-pin connector your stock light used. No adapter cord, no cutting, no splicing, no fuses.

03

Close the door. Open it again.

Your badge lands on the ground exactly where the factory courtesy light used to throw a blurry white blob.

What you'll need

  • A plastic trim/pry tool (the kind that ships in most interior tool kits)
  • About 5 minutes for a 2-door kit, 10 for a 4-door kit
  • Nothing else — no soldering iron, no wire strippers, no adapter harness
