Warranty & returns

12-month warranty

Every kit is covered for 12 months from the date of purchase against manufacturing defects. If a unit arrives faulty or fails under normal use, email info@mdivisionlighting.com with your order number and photos or a short clip, and we'll sort out a replacement.

Returns

Unused, uninstalled kits can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Email us to start a return. Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the kit arrived faulty or we sent the wrong item.

What's not covered

Damage from improper installation, modification, or water intrusion beyond the factory door-light housing's normal rating isn't covered under warranty.
